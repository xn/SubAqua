# Structural turn floor: which of the 36 paid turns no resource can remove

Reference: `docs/gold-star-run.txt` (SubAqua 2026-09-06, Pastamancer, mid tier, 36 turns).
Method: `2026-09-02-turn-ledger/ledger.py` splits the log at every `[N]` marker; 149 blocks,
36 paid. Each paid turn is matched to the quest step it advanced, then classified:

- **NC** — a turn-taking non-combat the quest step requires. Forcers pick _when_ it appears,
  never whether it costs a turn.
- **boss** — a fight the free-kill ladder cannot end (instakill-immune or `freeKillNever`).
- **route** — a turn the runplan chooses to spend for a resource payoff; another tier or a
  different routing skips it.
- **variance** — a wrong NC, a wrong tent location, or a random-order item.
- **luck** — a turn that was _not_ paid because bat wings' after-battle proc fired.

## Situations beyond "NCs and bosses"

1. **Fixed NC progressions.** Trench (3), Outpost colours (4) and the skate war (4) are
   sequences, not lotteries: every step is a paid NC and a forcer only removes the fights
   between them.
2. **Random-location and random-order NCs.** Tent (1 of 3), gym (2 needed of 5), pantry
   (1 of several), Picking Sides (one roll of the skate war pool). Floor 1/2/1, expected value higher; this run paid +2 at the tent and +1 in
   the pantry and rolled the gym perfectly.
3. **Capped free kill on mandatory rounds.** The Colosseum's 15 rounds are all combats, all
   instakill-immune, with exactly one 5/day free kill. This is the largest block of paid turns
   that is neither an NC nor a boss.
4. **Scheduled fight.** Peanut is instakill-immune and scheduled: a free run or banish only
   moves him to the next Abyss adventure. A waffle is the one thing that removes the turn: the
   fight continues against a re-rolled eye, which is free-killable and worth the same progress.
   Without a waffle he is a fourth boss.
5. **Bat wings are the only lever on bosses.** The after-battle proc has made Yog-Urt and the
   Seaceress free in logged runs. It is luck, not a budget.
6. **Fishy is assumed.** Every underwater paid turn above assumes Fishy is up; without it the
   turn costs double. The skate war is mandatory in the path, and its Lutz (30 turns of Fishy)
   went unclaimed in this run because pipe, fish sauce, PYEC and waves already covered 48.
